02 · My L5/S1 disc bulge story

Written by Conor O'Shea · Founder & Coach

I was told never to squat again.

A backbend injury caused an L5-S1 disc bulge, and doctors said squatting was over for good. After years of the wrong treatments, I built the Reset, Restore, Reinforce system now used with over 400 clients since 2011.

I damaged my L5-S1 doing a backbend, coaching a 6am client. It ruined my life for four years.

My doctor told me never to squat again. I was 28, a personal trainer, and it felt like someone had canceled my entire future.

I spent thousands on physios and coaches. It all helped for a while, then the pain kept coming back.

Eventually I found the right people, the ones who actually fixed it, plus thousands of hours of my own research. That's when I realized the low back isn't just a physical issue - it's also about how I respond to pain.

The worst part of those four years wasn't the pain itself. It was the anxiety, the fear of what it would do to my life, my career, my ability to provide as I got older.

That's what pushed me to help other people rehab their own backs once I'd solved mine.

Everything here is the piece of the puzzle I wish someone had handed me back in 2018, when I first hurt my back - refined through the years since as I keep coaching and learning from every new client. I built the process I've now used with over 400 clients since 2011.

Three phases, done in order. One outcome: your back stops running your life.

03 · The Method

The 3-step rehab system.

The 3-step system that takes you from chronic low back pain to a strong, reliable back: Reset calms the system down, Restore rebuilds the movement you're avoiding, Reinforce returns you to real training.

01

Reset

Drop the volume, drop the intensity, calm the system down. Rebuild the foundation from step one, not step five. This is the phase most people skip.

  • Drop the volume and intensity
  • Calm the nervous system down
  • Rebuild the foundation from step one
02

Restore

Bring back the movement your body has been avoiding. Load the tissue in the direction it fears. Graded exposure, not white-knuckle rehab.

  • Bring back the movement your body has been avoiding
  • Load the tissue in the direction it fears
  • Graded exposure, not white-knuckle rehab
03

Reinforce

Train the way you actually want to. Squats, deadlifts, running, sport. The same movements you were told to stop, now with the capacity to handle them.

  • Train the way you actually want to: squats, deadlifts, running, sport
  • Return to the movements you were told to stop
  • Build the capacity to handle them long-term

06 · Common questions

Common questions.

Does the program only train my lower back?

No. We train your whole body, because you're a human being, not a spine. The low back is usually the biggest focus, but it's rarely the only thing that needs addressing. Most clients have other injuries going on too, the back is just the one compromising their life the most. So we look after all of your training across the week. That's also essential from a load management standpoint, which is a huge part of low back rehab.

Can chronic low back pain heal without surgery?

In most cases, yes. Disc bulges and herniations have a much higher rate of natural resolution than most people are told. The disc gets its nutrition through movement, so we restore safe, graded movement rather than rest and avoid.

Is strength training safe with a herniated disc?

Once you've rebuilt the foundation, yes. Strength training is usually what closes the loop. The mistake most people make is going back to loaded lifts too early, on a base that can't hold them.

How long does rehab actually take?

Usually somewhere between the three and six month mark, but really, it depends. It's hard to give a specific number, because everyone's history is very different when they start the program.

Why does my back pain keep coming back?

Because the treatment fixed the symptom, not the pattern. The same weak links that caused it the first time cause it again. The loop breaks when you rebuild the base underneath the movement.

Do I need to stop lifting weights?

Almost never for good. Almost always for a phase:

  • Reset drops intensity so the tissue can heal
  • Restore rebuilds the foundation
  • Reinforce loads you back up to whatever your life demands

What if I'm nervous about rehabbing my back remotely?

This is one of the most common concerns clients raise. The reality is that if in-person was the answer, you'd probably have gotten results from someone in person already. More importantly, over 90% of low back pain is what's called non-specific back pain. That means it's driven by a multitude of factors, not a single adjustment or realignment that needs doing. There are several things at once holding your back back. That's exactly where a fully comprehensive approach comes in, looking at every factor in your lifestyle specifically to rehabilitate your back properly.

Do you work with clients outside Ireland?

Yes. The coaching runs remotely. Weekly check-ins, personalised programming, video review of your movements. Clients in the UK, US, Europe, and Australia.
