Questions

What people ask before they book.

I've tried physio, chiro and online programs. Why would this be different?

Most of our clients had tried 3 to 5 things before us. Those approaches treat the symptom or hand you a generic plan. We assess why your back keeps flaring up, build the foundation first, then progress you week by week and adjust when life gets in the way.

What if I flare up during the program?

Flare-ups happen, and they're not a sign it isn't working. You message your coach, we adjust your plan that day, and you keep moving forward instead of starting again from scratch.

How much time does it take?

3 to 4 sessions a week, usually 30 to 40 minutes. We start lower than you'd expect and build up, which is what stops the push-too-hard, flare-up, restart cycle.

Do I need a gym?

No. We build the plan around what you have. Plenty of clients start at home with minimal equipment and add the gym back in later.

I've had a scan showing a disc bulge or herniation. Can you still help?

Yes. Most of our clients come in with a disc injury, often L4/L5 or L5/S1. We work alongside your doctor, not instead of them. If anything on the call suggests you need medical care first, we'll tell you.

It's online. How do you coach me properly?

You film key sessions, your coach reviews your form, and you get feedback and a progressed plan every week. You also get direct messaging with your coach, so you're never guessing.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the level of support you need. We'll go through the options on the call, once we know whether we can actually help you.
